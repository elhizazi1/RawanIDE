# RawanIDE Support

- **Help and documentation:** https://rawanide.elhizazi.me/help
- **Report a problem or request a feature:** https://github.com/elhizazi1/RawanIDE/issues
- **APK releases:** https://github.com/elhizazi1/RawanIDE/releases
- **Privacy information:** https://rawanide.elhizazi.me/privacy

When reporting a problem, include the RawanIDE version, Android version, steps to reproduce, expected and actual behavior, and relevant sanitized logs. For optional integrations, mention whether Termux, Shizuku, Dhizuku, or an extension is involved.

Do not publish passwords, access tokens, private projects, or personal data.
